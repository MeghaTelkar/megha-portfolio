What Lab to Lucre does

Lab to Lucre is a Bengaluru consultancy for early-stage technology and life-science startups. It works with founders from prototype to first customer to first cheque.

Funding strategy

The right kind of capital for your stage, sized and sequenced so you raise with a plan.

Investor readiness

Story, deck, numbers and answers ready before you meet an investor.

Go-to-market

Positioning, pilots and first customers, so traction shows up before the term sheet.

1:1 advisory

Working sessions on your specific round, market and next 90 days.

What you get after a 1:1

  • Readiness scorecard. Where you stand on team, story, unit economics, market and traction, scored out of 20 each.
  • Funding route. Which kind of money fits your stage, in what order, and what proof unlocks each step.
  • Go-to-market plan. Your first buyer, entry offer, pricing test and the pilots that make investors listen.
  • Pitch feedback. Slide by slide: what to keep, what to fix.
  • A 90-day plan. Actions with owners and outputs, reviewed at week 6.

How fundraising help usually works

  • Most advisors charge a monthly retainer plus a success fee of about 1% to 3% of the money raised, and some charge much more.
  • Many start working with companies at Series A, after the hardest stretch is over.
  • Advisors with deep life-science knowledge mostly work for investors in London and Boston, not for Indian founders.
  • Founders say their biggest worry is paying upfront for investor access with no clear deliverables.

    About the Founder

    Lab to Lucre was founded by Megha Telkar, a biotech engineer with a marketing MBA, to help early-stage technology and life-science startups get from a working prototype to their first customer and first cheque.

    Megha spent more than two years in the Bengaluru startup and biotechnology ecosystem as Officer for Innovation and Ecosystem Development at Bangalore Bioinnovation Centre. There she supported life sciences, MedTech and agri-biotech startups through incubation, mentorship, business development and access to grants, angel investors and venture capital.

    At BIRAC she analysed about 10,000 startups and independently built a strategic funding playbook that helps investors and grant providers judge startup potential faster and more consistently. Lab to Lucre uses that research and those contacts with every founder it takes on.

    Questions founders ask

    Who is Lab to Lucre for?

    Early-stage founders in biotech, life sciences, MedTech, agri-biotech and other technology startups, from a working prototype to the first raise.

    What do I get from a 1:1?

    A written readiness report within 5 working days and a 90-day plan covering your funding route, investor story and go-to-market.

    Do you guarantee funding?

    No. Investors make their own decisions, and anyone who guarantees funding should be a red flag. Lab to Lucre gets you ready and shows you the right route.

    Do you sell investor lists or charge to pitch?

    No. What you get is the written report and the 90-day plan.
