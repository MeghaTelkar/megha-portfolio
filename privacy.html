Privacy policy

Last updated 2 November 2026

This page explains what the Lab to Lucre website collects, why, and what you can ask for. Lab to Lucre is run by Megha Telkar in Bengaluru, India. Questions go to telkar.megha24@gmail.com.

What the site records when you visit

  • Pages and sections viewed, buttons and links clicked, how far you scroll and how long you stay.
  • Device type, browser, operating system, screen size, language and time zone.
  • Where you came from (the referring site or campaign link).
  • A random visitor ID stored in your browser, so repeat visits can be counted. It is not linked to your name.

This data is anonymous unless you also send the contact form. If your browser sends a "Do Not Track" signal, the site records none of it.

What the contact form collects

Your name, email, company name (optional), role, the services you pick, your startup's stage and your message. The form is only sent after you tick the consent box.

The readiness check

Your answers are scored inside your browser and are not sent anywhere. If you click "Get a plan for these gaps", your score is copied into the contact form message, which you can edit or delete before sending.

How it is used

  • To reply to your query and follow up on it.
  • To understand which parts of the site are useful and improve them.

Your details are never sold, rented or shared for advertising.

Where it is stored

Visit data and form entries are saved to a private Google Sheet through Google Apps Script, so they are held on Google's servers. Only Megha has access. Web fonts on this site load from Google Fonts.

Your choices

  • Ask to see, correct or delete the details you sent by emailing telkar.megha24@gmail.com.
  • Clear your browser's site data to remove the visitor ID, or turn on "Do Not Track" to stop visit recording.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change too.
